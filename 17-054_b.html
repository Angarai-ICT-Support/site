ID 17-054 (B)
# of Openings 1
Job Location Alexandria, Virginia
Posted Date 07/27/2017
Closure Date 08/21/2017

Job Description:

  • Review the results of testing and simulation
  • Manage business vocabulary
  • Resolve business issues relating to business rule execution.
  • Be accountable for the quality of the business rule.

Requirements:

Experience:
  • Seven (7) or more years of functional expertise in SNAP certification policy.
  • Able to analyze complex problems, make recommendations, develop alternative solutions and draft and execute implementation plans. Able to converse with and provides assistance to highest levels of management.
  • Must be well-read and published in subject field.
  • A minimum of five (5) years of experience developing curriculum and or technical guides and providing training.
  • A minimum of five (5) years of communication experience facilitating meetings and providing expert consultation in the area of SNAP or related policy areas.
