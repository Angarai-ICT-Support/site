ANGARAI is a professional management consulting firm with a Project Management Office (PMO), specializing in Enterprise Transformation Services and Enterprise Oversight Services.

ABOUT US

Since being established in 2003 and based in Greenbelt, Maryland, ANGARAI has been recognized as one of the fastest growing small businesses in Prince George's County. As an ISO 9001 and CMMISM certified organization, we provide holistic solutions to our global customers through strategic business advice, organizational change management and enterprise technology development that includes both business and architecture solutions.

ANGARAI-provided solution sets include engagement activities such as project, program, portfolio, and change and risk management, with an emphasis on business transformation. Our critical success factors are drawn from a highly educated pool of professionals with a demonstrated experience and expertise in our key competency areas. A 360⁰ delivery philosophy integrates ANGARAI, its clients, and partners in the endeavor for collective excellence. We are experts in developing and/or adopting industry best practices that enable our clients to realize significant value. Focused on identifying pragmatic, reliable, dependable, and scalable solutions, we consistently exceed our clients' expectations.

VISION

Our vision is to be recognized as the premier provider of value-based holistic management solutions to our customers and be a catalyst and responsible corporate citizen for progressive growth in the communities where we work and provide our service.

MISSION

  • Support our customers with holistic management solutions that effectively address the goals and objectives of the organization with a customer-centric approach that results in long-term benefits and value and organizational growth and effectiveness.
  • Empower our employees, who are our ambassadors, with a progressive environment, purpose, resources and tools to assist their personal and professional growth and development, resulting in increased contribution to the firm and our customers.
  • Be the industry icon for best practices and provide a value proposition and service to customers with passion.
  • Create responsible corporate citizens to build a society that is progressive, inclusive and sustainable.
