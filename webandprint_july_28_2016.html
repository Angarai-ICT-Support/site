ANGARAI awarded HUBZone Certification by U.S. Small Business Administration

The U.S. Small Business Administration (SBA) awarded Angarai International, Inc. its HUBZone Certification on July 28, 2016.

The Historically Underutilized Business Zones (HUBZone) program was started by the SBA in 1998 in an effort to help small growing businesses in urban and rural communities gain equal access to federal procurement opportunities, thereby being able to operate and create jobs in communities with statistically proven economic needs and significant potential for growth and development. Today there are about 2450 recognized HubZones nationwide, with approximately 5000 small business participants.
