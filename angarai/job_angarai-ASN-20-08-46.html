Business Analyst

ID 20-08-46
# of Openings 1
Job Location Baltimore, MD
Posted Date 07-Apr-23
Closure Date 18-Apr-23

Job Description

ANGARAI is looking for a highly competent Business Analyst to support a Government Agency.

Major Roles and Responsibilities:

The Business Analyst shall work directly with the project manager, the project team, and State personnel and collaboratively work with the project stakeholder groups to:

  • Build requirements for a system using interviews, document analysis, requirements workshops, business process descriptions, use cases, scenarios, business analysis, task and workflow analysis using charts and diagrams.
  • Evaluate information gathered from multiple sources to reconcile requirements conflicts, decompose high level requirements information into detail requirements, and distinguish user requests from the underlying existing needs.
  • Communicate and collaborate with external and internal project stakeholders to analyze information needs and functional requirements and deliver the State SDLC artifacts.
  • Implement and apply best practices such as the International Institute of Business Analysis (IIBA) Business Analysis Body of Knowledge (BABOK) in requirements definition and management.
  • Document requirements in acceptable industry standards.
  • Study current system capabilities, identifying improvement in current system processes and functionality in order to recommend modifications in preparation for major system installations.
  • Recommend controls by identifying existing problems to write to-be procedures.
  • Prepare milestone status reports and deliveries/presentations on the system concept for colleagues, subordinates, and end user representatives.
  • Create user guides and functional and non-functional requirement specifications of current and future systems.
  • Assist with authoring solicitations.
  • Develop and maintain test plans, cases, and scripts.
  • Conduct functional testing prior to user acceptance testing (UAT).

Minimum Qualification & Experience:

  • Bachelor’s Degree from an accredited college or university in Computer Science, Information Systems, Engineering, Business, or other related scientific or technical discipline.

General Experience:

  • Must have five (5) years of computer experience working independently or under general direction on complex large-scale cloud-based applications.
  • Demonstrate an understanding of common challenges and how to document application development or software implementations to meet requirements for reports, interfaces, data conversion and added functionality.
  • Possess a strong understanding of the complete systems development lifecycle from project inception through system operations and maintenance.
  • Excellent business and analysis skills including past training in IIBA, BABOK or equivalent industry training.
  • Excellent oral, written, and interpersonal communication skills.
