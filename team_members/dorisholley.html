Doris Holley
COE Manager – Health & Human Services

Doris Holley is Director - Health and Human Services Solutions. Her duties include business development and program leadership pursuits in support of Federal, State and private Health and Human Services entities. Her direct experience includes all delivery and operational aspects of health care technology, including hospitals, integrated delivery systems, physician practices, emergency medicine, rehabilitation facilities, cancer centers, clinical research centers, acute care centers, pharmaceutical companies, insurance, human services and health maintenance organizations. Doris's comprehensive background includes a wide range of experience including healthcare IT sales, marketing, operations management, strategic planning, integrated systems planning, complex implementation management, business continuity, disaster recovery, tactical planning, health data management, system selections, risk management, process standards, assessments, data conversions, BI, business process reengineering, quality management, e-health and electronic medical records.

She has served in numerous executive and senior management positions as Vice President, CTO and Chief Operating Officer. Doris is a healthcare technology leader with experience in crafting strategic sales and marketing vision to achieve business goals. Her focus is in providing innovative solutions for Health and Human services that provide strong operational improvement and clear impressive Return on Investment (ROI's) by building responsive IT organizations and teams that consistently delivery operational and sales results through strong alignment of business goals and use of standardized processes.
