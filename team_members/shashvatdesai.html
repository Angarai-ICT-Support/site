Shashvat Desai, BE, MBA, PMP, ITIL
Business Execution Officer



Shashvat Desai has been with ANGARAI for almost four years now, during which he has held multiple roles, including leading ANGARAI’s Quality Management practice, and managing overall Corporate Operations.

He currently leads the Business Execution vertical at ANGARAI in which he is primarily responsible for the alignment of ANGARAI’s business processes with leadership objectives, and the development & implementation of strategic initiatives towards that effort. He leads projects that necessitate cross-functional dependencies and require involvement of multiple levels within the organization. He also manages all client engagements and is responsible for best-in-class service delivery and overall customer satisfaction.

Shashvat also oversees the development, institutionalization and maintenance of the ANGARAI QMS – a comprehensive Quality Management System that includes ANGARAI’s industry certifications like ISO9001:2008 and CMMISM, its operational best practices for both internal and external use, and its Body of Knowledge (ABOK) which holds all intellectual property of the company.

Shashvat holds a Bachelor’s degree in Telecommunications Engineering from the University of Mumbai, and an MBA in Strategy & International Business from Washington University. He is also a member of the "Hindu Anath Ashram", a home for under-privileged children in his home-town in India. In his leisure Shashvat enjoys trekking, hiking, boating and swimming. He also maintains a regular gym routine, and has a black belt in karate, Shotukan art form.
